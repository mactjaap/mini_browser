Mini Browser










Welcome to Mini Browser, a compact web browser for the WHY2025 badge.
It is designed to explore lightweight and server-rendered websites while staying small
enough to run comfortably on the badge.

The latest stable release is Mini Browser 3.0. It supports links, bookmarks,
Back/Forward history, simple GET and POST forms, bounded cookie support, PNG/JPEG/GIF
images, inline image rendering, multiple display modes, 56,352 Unicode glyphs,
word-aware wrapping, RTL/Bidi text, Arabic shaping, monochrome emoji, real bold text,
HTTP/TLS page information and full-page screenshots.





Mini Browser 4.3 is in development (4.3-dev1). Version 4.1 added an omnibox: one bar
for web addresses and searches, with suggestions from your bookmarks and history while you
type, a persistent history that survives a restart, and up to five tabs. Version 4.3 shows
loading progress, lets you stop a page with Esc, explains failed pages clearly, and brings
the last pages back instantly with Back and Forward.

How to browse




Links and form controls have numbers. Type a number and press Enter to activate it.
Use Up and Down to scroll; hold an arrow for continuous scrolling.
J and K also scroll one line at a time.





Press WHY+L and type a web address or just some words. An address such as wiby.me
opens directly; words such as esp32 badge become a Google search. While you type,
matching bookmarks and visited pages appear under the bar: use Down and Up to pick one
and Enter to open it, or Esc to cancel.





WHY+T opens a new tab with your bookmarks and recent pages. WHY+Tab or WHY+1 to WHY+5
switch tabs, WHY+A shows all tabs and WHY+W closes one.





While a page loads, the bar shows its progress; press Esc to stop. If a page cannot be
loaded, an error page tells you why; press R or Enter to try again.

Screenshots




Press WHY+S to capture the visible 716x716 Mini Browser view,
or WHY+Z to capture the complete rendered page.
Screenshots are transmitted over USB serial.
The repository includes badge_screenshot.py, a standalone macOS/Linux receiver
that validates the CRC/FEC-protected transfer and saves it as a PNG.




./badge_screenshot.py /dev/cu.wchusbserial10
./badge_screenshot.py /dev/cu.wchusbserial10 --full-page





Physical WHY+S and WHY+Z screenshots do not require custom firmware.
An optional customized WHY2025 firmware adds host-to-badge keyboard control,
so a computer can operate Mini Browser remotely.
It also includes scripts for automated browser tests and automatic screenshots.

About Mini Browser




Mini Browser 3.0 combines a built-in ASCII font with an external GNU Unifont-based
MBCJ font containing 56,352 usable glyphs across 101 ranges.
Text wrapping preserves complete words where possible, while CJK and
overlong tokens safely fall back to glyph wrapping.
Hebrew and Arabic are rendered right-to-left, with contextual Arabic shaping.
HTML b and strong text are rendered as genuine bold text.





Unordered list markers deliberately remain ASCII * characters,
so basic list rendering does not depend on the external Unicode font.





Mini Browser intentionally has no JavaScript engine, CSS layout engine or full HTML DOM.
Version 3.0 adds bounded PNG, JPEG and GIF image rendering, including inline images and
multiple display modes, while retaining the browser's focus on ordinary server-rendered
HTML, text content, links and simple GET and POST forms.
